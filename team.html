Leadership & Operating Team

The team building the platform.

SDR Drone Inc. is led by three principals who together carry three decades of unmanned-aviation engineering, defense procurement experience, and global commercialization expertise — supported by SUNDORI Co., Ltd., the Korean engineering and manufacturing team behind every platform.

Principals

The principals behind the platform.

Two Co-Chief Executives at the apex, an independent-director board overseeing governance, four operating leaders, and the Korean engineering bench — the leadership of SDR Drone Inc. brings together technology, programs, capital, governance, and global execution under one platform.

Office of the Co-Chief Executives
Apex · Two Co-CEOs
Co-Chief Executive · Founder

Sun-Sik Cho

Co-CEO · Founder & Platform Architect

Co-Chief Executive Officer, Founder, and Platform Architect of SDR Drone Inc. and SUNDORI Co., Ltd. Three decades of unmanned-aviation engineering — from Korea's first commercial unmanned airships in 1991 through the development of the SDR Multi Flight Control system, the SDR-ONE integrated avionics board, and today's defense-standard drone platform. Shares board-level executive authority with the Co-CEO, directing the company's technology roadmap, R&D, and engineering platform strategy.

Co-Chief Executive · Founding Partner

Leo Chung

Co-CEO · Founding Partner

Co-Chief Executive Officer and Founding Partner of SDR Drone Inc. Leads global business strategy, international defense-program engagements, and government partnerships across nine markets — including the United States, Japan, India, Indonesia, Thailand, Mongolia, Bangladesh, Cambodia, and the Republic of Korea. Shares board-level executive authority with the Founder-CEO across capital strategy, public-company governance, and long-horizon commercial positioning. Responsible for procurement pathways and technology-transfer frameworks.

Engineering Leadership Bench
SUNDORI Co., Ltd. · TECH Division
R&D Institute

Jung-Hwan Kim

Director · R&D Institute

Directs the R&D Institute — airframe design, avionics integration, and end-to-end product development. Principal authority on the technology roadmap and core platform architecture across rotary-wing and VTOL drones. Sets engineering priorities, approves design specifications for every production platform, and signs off on technical readiness before programs move from prototype to manufacturing.

Development & Production

Jong-Bae Kim

Director · Development & Production

Directs new-platform development and defense-program delivery at the Hanam facility. Owns design optimization — weight, payload integration, field-condition hardening — and manufacturing process control from prototype through serial production. Bridges R&D outputs to manufacturing reality, ensuring designs meet production yield, cost, and field-durability targets demanded by defense customers.

Software & Autonomy

Joon-Hwan Lee

Head of Software · Autonomy & Flight Systems

Owns the entire software stack across SDR Drone Inc. and SUNDORI Co., Ltd. — from onboard flight controllers and ground-control operating programs to multi-drone coordination and AI-based autonomy. Architect of the SDR Multi-Flight Control (MFS) suite and the single point of authority for all software architecture decisions across the platform. Designs 1:N swarm-control and AI autonomous-flight algorithms covering formation flight, leader-follower tracking, FANET mesh communications, and collision avoidance. Every line of flight, control, and autonomy code shipped on an SDR drone is built, reviewed, or directly authored under his ownership.

Engineering Operations

Si-On Jang

Manager · Engineering Operations & Certification

Manages the R&D Institute's design-to-build operations — 2D/3D mechanical drafting, 3D-printed airframe fabrication, BOM and inventory control, and airframe assembly with after-sales support. Handles KIAST safety certifications and Drone One-Stop registrations, and instructs SDR's drone operator training programs. Operational anchor that converts engineering drawings into deliverable hardware on tight defense-program timelines.

Production & Avionics

Jun-Young Kim

Manager · Production & Avionics Assembly

Production manager focused on drone airframe assembly and onboard electronics integration. Six-plus years at SUNDORI across special-purpose drone manufacturing and R&D delivery. Certified across three Korean aviation-maintenance trades — airframe, engine, and equipment — and holds an Ultralight UAV operator license. Final touchpoint between assembled platform and field deployment, responsible for quality control and acceptance testing on every unit that leaves the Hanam facility.
